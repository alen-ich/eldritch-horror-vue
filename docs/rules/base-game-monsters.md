# Base-game Monster spawning

The server-private Monster cup supports a deterministic internal draw-and-place transition. It consumes one physical normal token, creates a distinct on-board instance, and publishes the resulting location without exposing cup order. Six printed spawn effects that move a Monster to a named space are represented as typed destinations. Wraith's printed instruction makes the Lead Investigator gain Cursed through the existing Condition lifecycle; an existing Blessed Condition cancels it, and an exhausted Cursed supply cannot prevent the Monster itself from spawning. Empty Monster cups and unknown spawn effects do not mutate game state. The transition is not yet invoked by Gate or Mythos spawning.
