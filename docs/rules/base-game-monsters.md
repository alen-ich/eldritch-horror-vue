# Base-game Monster spawning

The server-private Monster cup supports a deterministic internal draw-and-place transition. It consumes one physical normal token, creates a distinct on-board instance, and publishes the resulting location without exposing cup order. Six printed spawn effects that move a Monster to a named space are represented as typed destinations. Wraith's printed instruction to give the Lead Investigator a Cursed Condition remains explicitly unsupported until that effect can be composed atomically with spawning. Empty cups and unsupported effects do not mutate game state. The transition is not yet invoked by Gate or Mythos spawning.
