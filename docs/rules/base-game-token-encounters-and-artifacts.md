Research sections are City/Wilderness/Sea; complex encounters are Initial/Pass/Fail. `artifacts.ts` catalogs all 14 Artifact names, printed traits, and mechanical-text transcriptions, replacing skill icons with their names. No free-form text is interpreted at runtime.

## Guarded Research engine slice

The authenticated `RESOLVE_RESEARCH_ENCOUNTER` command accepts only an opaque on-space Clue marker ID. Setup shuffles the selected Ancient One's available Research catalog into a private deck. Azathoth and Cthulhu now have transcribed rules; all 24 Azathoth and 18 Cthulhu sections are supported. The entire selected section is checked before rolling or consuming components. Sixteen scan-verified Assets now have explicit non-combat applicability: nine have no relevant effect; Urban Guide supplies its same-City additional die; Personal Assistant supplies its holder's Influence bonus and optional free reroll; Lucky Rabbit's Foot supplies an optional once-per-round test reroll; Hired Muscle supplies its holder's Strength bonus and optional Strength reroll; Bandages supplies optional same-space ordinary Health-loss prevention by physical discard; Whiskey similarly prevents Sanity loss. Witch Doctor and Puzzle Box are Rest-only and have no relevant effect here. Lucky Cigarette Case supplies optional once-per-round +1 to one test die result (1–5 only), independently of rerolls. Source-specific declines and both-resource reactions precede simultaneous damage. Item-discard branches support conservation-checked choices among eligible held Items. Paid Health costs with eligible Bandages, paid Sanity costs with eligible Whiskey, other held Assets and ambush possession/aura effects remain guarded; see `base-game-assets.md`.

Supported sections reuse the ordered location instruction continuation, physical Condition/damage/Delay/spawn effects, and owner-only test rerolls. `encountered_clue` binds to the chosen exact marker; extra Clues draw from supply independently. Failed tests leave uncollected Clues on the board. An unconditional Clue grant before a test makes that token immediately available to reroll that later test. The card stays on top while decisions are pending, cycles once on completion or defeat, and advances the encounter seat after the existing defeat lifecycle. Only the current/last revealed card and public marker state synchronize; the queue, physical identities, and undrawn order remain private.

The browser exposes Research buttons for on-space markers and displays the revealed Research artwork. Unsupported sections report an error without changing the card or Clue. Engine tests cover exact-token conservation, retention, modifiers, prefix ordering, serializable owner-only rerolls, deck cycling, unsupported preflight, and authorization. A two-player room test verifies command ownership, hidden data, continuation round trips, and phase handoff. The existing multiplayer browser journey also passes; it is not a dedicated Research browser test.

Research Omen movement uses the shared owner-only Encounter choice with four physical destinations, including both blue spaces and the current space. It never advances Doom or triggers Azathoth's advance-only green effect. Printed advancement reuses the same internal resolver as Mythos: matching Gates plus Azathoth's green Eldritch tokens count on entering green, with immediate defeat at zero Doom. The exported Mythos operation still rejects Encounter timing. Placement increases `greenOmenTokens` by one without immediate Doom loss. Removal discards one from green, or does nothing at zero; every modeled base Azathoth source places Omen Eldritch tokens on green, so it is the only occupied space and no selection is needed. Generalized token placement on other Omen spaces is not modeled. Tests cover these distinctions, zero-token removal, invalid Omen state, game-ending advancement, and owner-only serialized multiplayer choices.

Card 5 City failure now chooses one solved Mystery using injected server randomness, removes it from `completedMysteryIds`, adds it to the remaining private Mystery deck, and reshuffles. The active Mystery, its target, and its current progress remain unchanged; the encountered Clue stays on the board after failure. With no solved Mysteries the return is a no-op (no Doom fallback and no selection/shuffle input consumed). Nonempty returns require a valid selected-AO catalog and private deck; duplicate physical identities, active/deck overlap, and unknown cards reject atomically. A returned Mystery's former public identity may appear in an event, but deck order does not. Four engine tests and a two-player room test cover conservation, random selection, success/no-op branches, privacy, and continued turn order.

Card 5 Sea now uses the existing owner-only optional encounter choice. Accepting gains a physical Dark Pact, collects the exact encountered Clue, then transfers T'tka Halot from its private supply. Declining retains the Clue. An unavailable or already-held Dark Pact cannot satisfy the cost. If the named Artifact is already held elsewhere, it cannot be gained again; the printed Clue reward still resolves. Invalid Artifact conservation rejects before offering and atomically on acceptance. The serialized continuation cycles the card once and advances the encounter seat, without exposing undrawn order or Condition reverse identities. Five engine regressions and a two-player room test cover these cases. Dark Pact's broader Reckoning/reverse lifecycle remains pending.

## Artifact physical foundation

Setup now shuffles all 14 unique Artifact identities into a server-private deck. Public investigator `artifactIds` holdings round-trip through the room, copy safely during engine transitions/projection, and render in an Artifact inventory tab with effects explicitly marked pending. Defeat moves held Artifacts to the defeated investigator's sheet; replacement begins without duplicating those cards.

Internal `gainNamedArtifact` transfers a supported single physical Artifact from supply while preserving the other cards' order. T'tka Halot, Cultes des Goules and De Vermis Mysteriis are supported (the latter two require verified typed effects). It validates conservation across supply, living holdings, and defeated sheets, and rejects duplicates, unknown identities, missing components, ended/lobby games, and defeated recipients. The other 11 Artifacts remain blocked because printed effects are not implemented. There is no client grant command or Artifact discard/trade handler. T'tka Halot is available through card 5 Sea and its action below.

Puzzle Box adds a guarded random-deck draw: its optional Rest Observation −2 test discards the Box and transfers the top Artifact on a pass. The shuffled top must be executable T'tka Halot, Cultes des Goules or De Vermis Mysteriis, or the test rejects before dice/discard without revealing that card's name; it never searches past an unsupported card. A conserved depleted deck grants nothing. The actor can skip the Box and finish Rest regardless of an unsupported next draw. The private Rest/test continuation preserves one-action counting, Clue/Asset die effects and independent Witch Doctor ordering. This is not full Artifact support; see `base-game-assets.md` for exact scope and tests.

T'tka Halot's Action now supports authoritative Lore testing/Clue rerolls, optional spending of 1 Sanity, and owner-private selection of an on-space Monster to lose 3 Health without combat rewards. Numeric toughness without unimplemented special abilities is supported, including partial Epic damage. Defeated normal tokens return to the cup unless set aside. Completion consumes one action and records once-per-physical-card use for the round. Carried Assets/other Artifacts guard unsupported test bonuses. Seven engine tests and a multiplayer test cover costs, refusal, failure, ownership, serialization, damage, conservation and turn handoff.

Cultes des Goules is scan-verified **Item — Tome** with typed `action_lore_spend_sanity_gain_clues` (amount 2), not parsed prose. `USE_ARTIFACT_ACTION` rolls Lore with improvements and Blessed/Cursed thresholds, allowing physical-Clue rerolls. After a final pass, the owner receives an optional `artifact_payment` choice. Acceptance spends 1 Sanity (never the last point) and gains up to two physical Clues from supply, recycling discards if needed; depleted supply cannot manufacture tokens. Board Clues are not collected or spawned. Failure, insufficient spendable Sanity and decline still consume one component action. The Artifact stays held, with independent per-card round usage.

The original physical Cultes source is revalidated before rolling, on resumed tests and on payment; Clue catalog conservation/holdings are checked before payment. Invalid or unauthorized commands leave the checkpoint unchanged. The sixteen explicitly supported non-combat Assets and supported action-only Tomes may coexist; unknown/unsupported possessions remain guarded. Twenty-seven new Cultes engine cases, a Puzzle Box top-Cultes regression and two real multiplayer restoration/payment cases cover its original slice. Family readiness remains `needs_review`, not fully executable.

De Vermis Mysteriis is scan-verified **Item — Tome** with typed `action_lore_spend_sanity_improve_skill`. Its authoritative Lore test and physical-Clue rerolls precede an owner-only `artifact_payment` decision offering five skills and decline. A valid skill choice atomically spends 1 Sanity (not the last point), improves that skill once, records this physical card's round use and consumes one action. Lily may instead choose twice, or retain once. Improvements cap at +2, including a selected already-capped skill; the printed cost still applies. Failed tests, insufficient spendable Sanity and decline finish without a reward/cost. The card stays held.

The common `validateTomeAction` rechecks the original source, phase, active owner, restrictions, remaining action and usage before rolling, at resumed tests and on payment. Skill/payment options are server-authored; a non-Lily double improvement, changed Lily identity, invented skill or insufficient Sanity rejects atomically. No new private state field or public reward command is added. Twenty-eight De Vermis engine cases, one true-top Puzzle Box regression and two room restoration/ownership cases cover this slice. Only 3/14 Artifact gain/effects are supported; readiness is unchanged.

The subsequent Asset integration now applies `validateArtifactTestAssets` to all three Tomes, allowing the sixteen explicit non-combat Assets, checking physical held/local-aura identities, and retaining unknown/unsupported/passive-Artifact guards. Lore combines improvements and local Urban Guide City aura; the shared Foot/Case and physical-Clue choices retain source limits and restored eligibility. No Influence/Strength/Spell/combat bonus is misapplied. Sanity payment is not preventable damage, so Whiskey cannot waive it. See `base-game-assets.md` for the 36 engine/two-room regressions and exact scope. Other World foundation status is recorded below.

## Other World foundation: The Future (card 2)

Its Initial failure loses 3 Sanity unless spending one Clue. Spell rewards are not executable, so the engine rejects the **whole card** before RNG or closure rather than deleting that option.

Initial failure instead tests Lore (0): pass has no effect, fail loses 6 Sanity. That entire failure branch leaves the Gate active, including when the Lore test succeeds. Lethal loss stops the card and enters the existing defeat flow; no later reward runs.

`RESOLVE_OTHER_WORLD_ENCOUNTER` accepts only the on-space Gate ID. The server chooses the actual private deck top and owns dice, nested branches and closure. All 24 imported physical card IDs are shuffled at setup, not just card 2. Unsupported tops reject without revealing, rotating or skipping them. New games receive this deck; an older room with no initialized deck rejects safely and needs a fresh game rather than inventing an order during play.

The shared `pendingLocationEncounter` queue privately binds the original Gate, location and closure status. Restored decisions recheck that binding, the current card, actual top, complete encounter deck, and Gate conservation across active/discarded/private stack. A closed Gate need not remain active for the follow-up test. Empty Gate stacks recycle discards privately, using existing component semantics. Closure never changes Doom, removes Monsters or reopens the token. Each test supports physical-Clue rerolls, improvements and Blessed/Cursed thresholds. The card cycles once and the seat advances only on completion or through the existing defeat lifecycle.

This deliberately narrow slice admits physically unique, scan-verified irrelevant Assets but guards applicable/unknown Assets and all Artifacts among living same-space holders, Akachi's optional post-closure movement, and Yog-Sothoth's closure/Spell/Mystery interactions. Ordinary non-combat possession applicability is not automatically assumed for Other Worlds. Cards 6 and 19–24 remain untranscribed/unexecutable; whole-family scan/readiness flags remain unchanged. The original thirty-one engine cases, three content cases, one protocol case and two real room cases cover ordering, failure retention, lethal loss, physical rerolls, private serialization/ownership, restored invalid sources and unsupported preflight. Setup additionally checks all-24 private shuffling. The browser exposes on-space Gate buttons and revealed artwork through existing dialogs.
