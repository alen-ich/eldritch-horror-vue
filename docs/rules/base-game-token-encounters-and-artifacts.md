`packages/content/src/remaining-card-scans.json` is the explicit visual import map shared by the content catalog and `pnpm remaining-cards:generate`. Numbers are printed card numbers, not source positions; the Himalayas front is ordered 2, 1, 3. Source misspellings such as `yog-sogoth` are retained as filenames but normalized to canonical `yog_sothoth` IDs. Individual Expedition group scans are canonical; duplicate combined `front-1.jpg`/`front-2.jpg` sheets are retained unchanged and are not imported twice.

Generated files live under `apps/web/public/cards/encounters/<deck>/` and `apps/web/public/cards/artifacts/`. These backs are shared deck-identification artwork, unlike private Spell/Condition reverse outcomes. Public image availability does not authorize synchronizing undrawn deck order or automatically resolving cards.

`token-encounters.ts` catalogs 86 faces with family, deck, printed number, section structure, display title, source sheet/cell, paired back, and stable image paths. Research sections are City/Wilderness/Sea; complex encounters are Initial/Pass/Fail. `artifacts.ts` catalogs all 14 Artifact names, printed traits, and mechanical-text transcriptions, replacing skill icons with their names. No free-form text is interpreted at runtime.

## Guarded Research engine slice

The authenticated `RESOLVE_RESEARCH_ENCOUNTER` command accepts only an opaque on-space Clue marker ID. Setup shuffles the selected Ancient One's available Research catalog into a private deck. Azathoth and Cthulhu now have transcribed rules; all 24 Azathoth and 18 Cthulhu sections are supported. The entire selected section is checked before rolling or consuming components. Thirteen scan-verified Assets now have explicit non-combat applicability: seven have no relevant effect; Urban Guide supplies its same-City additional die; Personal Assistant supplies its holder's Influence bonus and optional free reroll; Lucky Rabbit's Foot supplies an optional once-per-round test reroll; Hired Muscle supplies its holder's Strength bonus and optional Strength reroll; Bandages supplies optional same-space ordinary Health-loss prevention by physical discard; Whiskey similarly prevents Sanity loss. Source-specific declines and both-resource reactions precede simultaneous damage. Item-discard branches support conservation-checked choices among eligible held Items. Paid Health costs with eligible Bandages, paid Sanity costs with eligible Whiskey, other held Assets and ambush possession/aura effects remain guarded; see `base-game-assets.md`.

Supported sections reuse the ordered location instruction continuation, physical Condition/damage/Delay/spawn effects, and owner-only test rerolls. `encountered_clue` binds to the chosen exact marker; extra Clues draw from supply independently. Failed tests leave uncollected Clues on the board. An unconditional Clue grant before a test makes that token immediately available to reroll that later test. The card stays on top while decisions are pending, cycles once on completion or defeat, and advances the encounter seat after the existing defeat lifecycle. Only the current/last revealed card and public marker state synchronize; the queue, physical identities, and undrawn order remain private.

The browser exposes Research buttons for on-space markers and displays the revealed Research artwork. Unsupported sections report an error without changing the card or Clue. Engine tests cover exact-token conservation, retention, modifiers, prefix ordering, serializable owner-only rerolls, deck cycling, unsupported preflight, and authorization. A two-player room test verifies command ownership, hidden data, continuation round trips, and phase handoff. The existing multiplayer browser journey also passes; it is not a dedicated Research browser test.

Research Omen movement uses the shared owner-only Encounter choice with four physical destinations, including both blue spaces and the current space. It never advances Doom or triggers Azathoth's advance-only green effect. Printed advancement reuses the same internal resolver as Mythos: matching Gates plus Azathoth's green Eldritch tokens count on entering green, with immediate defeat at zero Doom. The exported Mythos operation still rejects Encounter timing. Placement increases `greenOmenTokens` by one without immediate Doom loss. Removal discards one from green, or does nothing at zero; every modeled base Azathoth source places Omen Eldritch tokens on green, so it is the only occupied space and no selection is needed. Generalized token placement on other Omen spaces is not modeled. Tests cover these distinctions, zero-token removal, invalid Omen state, game-ending advancement, and owner-only serialized multiplayer choices.

Card 5 City failure now chooses one solved Mystery using injected server randomness, removes it from `completedMysteryIds`, adds it to the remaining private Mystery deck, and reshuffles. The active Mystery, its target, and its current progress remain unchanged; the encountered Clue stays on the board after failure. With no solved Mysteries the return is a no-op (no Doom fallback and no selection/shuffle input consumed). Nonempty returns require a valid selected-AO catalog and private deck; duplicate physical identities, active/deck overlap, and unknown cards reject atomically. A returned Mystery's former public identity may appear in an event, but deck order does not. Four engine tests and a two-player room test cover conservation, random selection, success/no-op branches, privacy, and continued turn order.

Card 5 Sea now uses the existing owner-only optional encounter choice. Accepting gains a physical Dark Pact, collects the exact encountered Clue, then transfers T'tka Halot from its private supply. Declining retains the Clue. An unavailable or already-held Dark Pact cannot satisfy the cost. If the named Artifact is already held elsewhere, it cannot be gained again; the printed Clue reward still resolves. Invalid Artifact conservation rejects before offering and atomically on acceptance. The serialized continuation cycles the card once and advances the encounter seat, without exposing undrawn order or Condition reverse identities. Five engine regressions and a two-player room test cover these cases. Dark Pact's broader Reckoning/reverse lifecycle remains pending.

## Artifact physical foundation

Setup now shuffles all 14 unique Artifact identities into a server-private deck. Public investigator `artifactIds` holdings round-trip through the room, copy safely during engine transitions/projection, and render in an Artifact inventory tab with effects explicitly marked pending. Defeat moves held Artifacts to the defeated investigator's sheet; replacement begins without duplicating those cards.

Internal `gainNamedArtifact` transfers the single T'tka Halot copy from supply while preserving the other cards' order. It validates conservation across supply, living holdings, and defeated sheets, and rejects duplicates, unknown identities, missing components, ended/lobby games, and defeated recipients. Other Artifacts are blocked because printed on-gain/passive effects are not implemented. There is no client grant command, random Artifact draw or Artifact discard/trade handler. T'tka Halot is now available through card 5 Sea and its action below.

T'tka Halot's Action now supports authoritative Lore testing/Clue rerolls, optional spending of 1 Sanity, and owner-private selection of an on-space Monster to lose 3 Health without combat rewards. Numeric toughness without unimplemented special abilities is supported, including partial Epic damage. Defeated normal tokens return to the cup unless set aside. Completion consumes one action and records once-per-physical-card use for the round. Carried Assets/other Artifacts guard unsupported test bonuses. Seven engine tests and a multiplayer test cover costs, refusal, failure, ownership, serialization, damage, conservation and turn handoff.

## Cthulhu Research

All eight cards (24 sections) are transcribed from their imported English crops in printed-number order. Typed nodes retain named Monster ambush outcomes, refusal consequences, Mists of Releh, Grotesque Statue and Ruby of R'lyeh rather than dropping unsupported text. Executable sections: 1 City; 2 all; 3 City/Wilderness; 4 all; 5 all; 6 Wilderness/Sea; 7 Wilderness/Sea; 8 City/Wilderness. The remaining six reject the whole section before dice/token consumption.

Nearest-Sea relocation measures path count over all map routes, not geographic proximity or ticket cost. It relocates only the encountered opaque marker, preserving its private physical identity. Equal shortest-distance destinations use a serializable owner-private choice and revalidate on acceptance. Retreat Doom adds 1, capped at the printed track's 20, without Omen movement/awakening. Printed Item discards now use successive mandatory owner choices, moving chosen physical Assets to the discard pile before relocation. Zero/insufficient Items discard only those available; Allies are excluded. Duplicate physical sources reject before rolling and on acceptance. Condition gains, ordered damage and Delay reuse existing handlers, including their current reverse-effect guards.

Two content tests, 19 engine tests (including both branches of every supported section), and a room test cover transcription, unsupported preflight, modifiers, rewards, nearest-Sea movement, ties/privacy/serialization, Doom boundaries, exact-token retention and card cycling. No family-wide executable claim is made: Cthulhu awakening, possession bonuses, named Spells/Artifacts and ambush combat still require implementation.

### Named Deep One ambush continuation

Reference Guide page 2 was re-extracted and visually checked: a named Monster ambush references attributes rather than drawing or spawning a physical token. This handler never changes the cup, set-aside supply or an existing board Monster, and never executes spawn/Reckoning effects. Will/Horror resolves before Strength/Damage, with improvements and Blessed/Cursed thresholds. Each test supports sequential physical-Clue rerolls and a serializable owner-private checkpoint. A new named-ambush result event records the final Strength result without implying that a board token was defeated.

The interpreter expands a supported ambush into ordered combat tests, then selects the printed defeated/not-defeated continuation. Investigator defeat at either stage terminates the card before rewards or failure Conditions; it cycles exactly once and enters the existing defeat lifecycle. Named-stat and unsupported possession guards run before the initial Research test. Cthulhu card 4 City and card 7 Sea are now enabled, raising coverage to 17/24. The other Deep One sections remain blocked by their named Artifact rewards. Eight additional engine cases and a room test cover the new sections, independent rerolls, serialization/ownership, both forms of defeat, thresholds, token preservation and missing-stat preflight. Ordinary board combat's Clue rerolls are not changed by this slice.

### Star Spawn ambush continuation

Cthulhu card 5 Sea is now enabled (18/24 sections). Successful Observation collects this Clue without combat. Failure retains it and resolves a named Star Spawn ambush using Will -1/Horror 3, Strength -3/Damage 3 and toughness 5. Victory retreats Doom by 1, capped at 20, but does not award the Clue; survival without victory leaves Doom unchanged. Investigator defeat interrupts the card and applies the normal defeat Doom penalty, not the victory reward. Star Spawn's Reckoning does not execute during an ambush, and its physical set-aside/cup/board tokens and existing damage remain untouched.

The same private serializable test continuation supports owner-only physical-Clue rerolls. Six additional engine cases cover the newly supported section, printed stats, victory/cap, failure, token conservation, rerolls, both defeat timings and preflight guards; a room test restores each of the Observation/Will/Strength checkpoints and rejects the other player's choices. Victory uses a synthetic high-Strength test investigator to isolate toughness rules; this does not enable pending possession combat bonuses for real investigators.
