# Base-game Condition scans

The supplied English sheets contain all **36 physical base-game Conditions**. Their printed front order is 32 cards on `front-1.jpg` and four on `front-2.jpg`; the two back sheets use the same row/column order. There are six Debt cards and three each of Amnesia, Dark Pact, Leg Injury, Internal Injury, Paranoia, Cursed, Hallucinations, Detained, Blessed, and Back Injury.

`packages/content/src/conditions.ts` assigns a stable ID to every physical copy, records the front name, trait, and mechanical summary, and preserves the source sheet and slot. `pnpm conditions:generate` creates 36 front crops at `apps/web/public/cards/conditions/` and 36 paired back crops at `apps/server/private-card-assets/conditions/`. The back crops must **not** be moved into `apps/web/public` or synchronized to other players. If a card flips during play, the server must authorize the reveal and send only the resulting owner-visible information.

All 36 distinct backs now have server-private mechanical transcriptions keyed by physical card ID in `apps/server/src/private-condition-backs.ts`. They are not exposed through the public content package or browser bundle. Canonical engine state and the room keep the physical copy ID private; synchronized player state, owner decisions, action targets, and events use only front-side Condition identities. The server initializes finite supplies and returns discarded cards to them. Bank Loan can award Debt during Acquire Assets, Rest offers optional recovery rolls for Injury/Madness, and the Debt/Detained local actions use authoritative Influence tests with Clue rerolls. Detained blocks other actions, and Blessed/Cursed change skill-test thresholds and handle opposite-kind cancellation. Defeat returns held Conditions to their supplies.

An internal Mythos-only resolver performs the verified Blessed/Cursed front-side Reckoning roll, retaining or discarding the physical card as printed. Injury/Madness fronts with a supported reverse now use the ordinary authoritative Strength/Will test, including reconnect-safe Clue rerolls; failure applies the reverse only after the final test result. Stabbing Pain makes the investigator Delayed and returns Leg Injury front-side. Delusions discards all Clues and the Hallucinations copy. The resolver is not a player command; only the narrow guarded Blessed/Cursed path is wired to Mythos icon sequencing, not live room play. Fronts without an executable reverse deliberately return an unsupported error without consuming dice or changing state.
The guarded yellow Mythos Reckoning step now invokes the Blessed/Cursed resolver in Lead Investigator order when no higher-priority or choice-dependent effects are present. Other Condition kinds remain blocked at that step.

The same-kind gain path can now resolve three atomic, server-supplied backs: Karmic Justice discards the held Cursed copy; Beloved of Bast recovers up to 2 Health and 2 Sanity and returns Blessed front-side; Visions of Hypnos grants 2 Clues and returns Blessed front-side. No duplicate copy is drawn. Their physical identities and typed effect descriptors remain server-side; the engine emits only public-kind events after resolution. Every other reverse remains explicitly unsupported.

The scanned **Everyone Has a Price** Mythos Event uses the existing finite Debt supply and discard path. In Lead order, each active investigator may gain a Debt to discard one previously held non-Debt Condition, or skip. An investigator who already holds Debt or cannot draw one cannot use the exchange. Decisions identify Conditions by public front kind only; physical copy IDs and reverse text remain private. The exchange returns the discarded copy to its matching supply and survives reconnection.

This is **still not a complete Condition implementation**. Other reverse effects still need typed executable handlers and choices. The Mythos/Reckoning and Encounter phase trigger points are not implemented yet, so Debt, Dark Pact, the remaining Injury/Madness backs, and Detained reverse resolution are not in live play. The family remains `needs_review` and `not_executable` until every printed reverse is resolved authoritatively.
