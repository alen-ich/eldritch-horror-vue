# Base-game Condition scans

The supplied English sheets contain all **36 physical base-game Conditions**. Their printed front order is 32 cards on `front-1.jpg` and four on `front-2.jpg`; the two back sheets use the same row/column order. There are six Debt cards and three each of Amnesia, Dark Pact, Leg Injury, Internal Injury, Paranoia, Cursed, Hallucinations, Detained, Blessed, and Back Injury.

`packages/content/src/conditions.ts` assigns a stable ID to every physical copy, records the front name, trait, and mechanical summary, and preserves the source sheet and slot. `pnpm conditions:generate` creates 36 front crops at `apps/web/public/cards/conditions/` and 36 paired back crops at `apps/server/private-card-assets/conditions/`. The back crops must **not** be moved into `apps/web/public` or synchronized to other players. If a card flips during play, the server must authorize the reveal and send only the resulting owner-visible information.

All 36 distinct backs now have server-private mechanical transcriptions keyed by physical card ID in `apps/server/src/private-condition-backs.ts`. They are not exposed through the public content package or browser bundle. Canonical engine state and the room keep the physical copy ID private; synchronized player state, owner decisions, action targets, and events use only front-side Condition identities. The server initializes finite supplies and returns discarded cards to them. Bank Loan can award Debt during Acquire Assets, Rest offers optional recovery rolls for Injury/Madness, and the Debt/Detained local actions use authoritative Influence tests with Clue rerolls. Detained blocks other actions, and Blessed/Cursed change skill-test thresholds and handle opposite-kind cancellation. Defeat returns held Conditions to their supplies.

This is **still not a complete Condition implementation**. Same-kind Blessed/Cursed gains and Reckoning may require a back flip; reverse effects still need typed executable handlers and choices. The Mythos/Reckoning and Encounter phase trigger points are not implemented yet, so Debt, Dark Pact, Injury/Madness, and Detained reverse resolution is not in live play. The family remains `needs_review` and `not_executable` until every printed reverse is resolved authoritatively.
