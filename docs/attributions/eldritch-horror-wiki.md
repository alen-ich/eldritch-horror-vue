# Eldritch Horror Wiki monster data and images

The base-game Monster catalog in `packages/content/src/monsters.ts` was transcribed from the Core entries in the Eldritch Horror Wiki's [normal Monster list](https://eldritchhorror.fandom.com/wiki/Monsters), [Epic Monster list](https://eldritchhorror.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Monsters), and linked individual Monster pages on 2026-09-27. Core-box counts were cross-checked against the supplied `eldritch_horror_base_monsters_ru.pdf` reference.

The 37 token images under `apps/web/public/monsters/` are local copies of the corresponding files linked by those wiki entries. The wiki marks community text as CC BY-SA; game names, artwork, and token designs remain the property of their respective rights holders. These assets are retained with stable source-page links in the structured catalog and are intended for this non-commercial game implementation.
